How it plays

One thumb, three moves.

  • 01

    Tap on the pulse

    Tap anywhere as the ring lands on the beat. A Perfect pushes the storm ring back the furthest, and a Good still holds it off.

  • 02

    Steer the shield

    Slide a finger around the planet to steer the shield and block the meteors coming in.

  • 03

    Hold for a shockwave

    Perfect taps fill a meter. When it is full, press and hold to release a shockwave.

  • 04

    Twelve worlds, twelve rules

    Pluto to Mortimus. Meteors arrive in volleys over the Moon, dust storms hide them on Mars, steering flips on Uranus, a debris belt circles Saturn, gales push your shield off line on Neptune and gravity bends every meteor toward you on Jupiter. Earn stars on a world to unlock the next. Each world has its own Game Center leaderboard, measured to the hundredth of a second.

  • 05

    The Daily Storm

    A fresh seeded storm every day, the same for every player, with one free attempt. Everyone plays on the same fixed gear, so its own leaderboard is a pure test of skill. Keep your streak alive, earn rewards the next day if you finish near the top, and see your streak on a home-screen widget.

  • 06

    Earn it, then use it

    Stardust from every run buys upgrades, capped at level 5 so every guardian can reach the same gear. Cores buy two one-use power-ups: Shield takes over the steering for 10 seconds, and Slo-Mo slows the meteors and the storm by 75% for 8 seconds.

Free to play

Everything to play is free.

Orbit Guard is free to download. Every world and the Daily Storm can be played without paying.

Free

Play and progress without paying.

  • All twelve worlds, unlocked with the stars you earn
  • The Daily Storm, with one free attempt a day
  • Upgrades earned with Stardust from every run
  • Rewarded ads are optional: continue a run, double your Stardust or get an extra Daily attempt
  • Short ads can appear between runs

Optional extras

In-app purchases, all optional.

  • Remove Ads, a one-time purchase that takes away the ads between runs
  • Cores packs, spent on power-ups, cosmetics and streak freezes
  • A Starter Pack, a one-time offer
  • Guardian Pass: Season 1, six weeks of extra rewards and an exclusive look

Cosmetics are purely visual. Purchases are handled by Apple, and you can restore them in the app’s Shop.

Questions

Good questions.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Game Center is optional and only used for the leaderboards and achievements.

What is the Daily Storm?

One seeded storm a day, the same for every player, with one free attempt. Everyone plays on the same fixed gear, so its leaderboard is a pure test of skill. It resets at 00:00 UTC.

Does paying put me ahead on the leaderboards?

Upgrades are earned by playing and capped, and power-ups are optional helpers you can use once per run in world runs. Neither exists in the Daily Storm, where everyone has the same gear, and cosmetics are purely visual.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The game plays without a connection. Ads, purchases and Game Center need one.

Is there a widget?

Yes. A home-screen widget shows today’s Daily Storm and your streak.

What does it need?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
